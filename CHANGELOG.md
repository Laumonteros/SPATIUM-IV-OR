# Changelog

[🇪🇸 Registro completo en español](docs/CHANGELOG.es.md)

## 1.0.0 — Tutorial, full campaign, helium-3 and the trip home
- Optional 8-step **tutorial** at the start of a new game.
- **13-mission campaign** (7 new): mapping, water in the shadows, helium-3, maintenance, solar storm with forecast and regolith shielding, bigger base, going home.
- **Helium-3**: new scarce resource; fuels an *experimental* cell (future technology, clearly labeled).
- **Propellant from ice** (electrolysis) and **lift-off** of the Ariel module, with an end-of-mission summary.
- **Solar forecast**: a radio warning before every flare.
- **Windows, macOS and Linux** downloads.

## 0.12.0 — Power budget and playable greenhouse
- Power budget: life support, hydroponics, science and comms at 100 % / 50 % / off, with real consequences and battery autonomy.
- Greenhouse: choose what to plant among 6 NASA-tested crops; harvest by hand.

## 0.11.0 — Random events, radio voice, decisions with a cost
- 7 random events (real DONKI flares, dust, hull breach, comm blackout, impact flash, moonquake, science request).
- Mission Control radio with voice in **English and Spanish**; decision dialogs showing gains and costs.
- Full language switch, including in-world signs.

## 0.10.0 — Resources, collecting, inventory
- Distinct **iron (ilmenite)** in veins; more resources; a **different resource layout every game**.
- Visual collecting feedback; **inventory** screen (Tab); supply capsules stay after unloading.

## 0.9.0 — Sky, planets, rock cards, scanner, map icons
- Procedural star field with depth; Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn with correct phases.
- Binocular data cards for Earth, planets and rocks; scanner marks rocks by what they yield; color-coded map icons.
- New main menu; Esc no longer quits the game; habitat exits fixed; 4-leg solar panels.

## 0.8.0 — Colony fixes, new Sun, Earth data, Mission Control voice
- Rover no longer sinks; seamless game start; radio voice; new Sun; Earth data card; module textures; fewer neon lights; readable signs; doors fixed.

## 0.7.0 · 0.6.0 · 0.5.0 — Pre-installed lunar colony (team design)
