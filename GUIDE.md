# SPATIUM IV — Player & judge guide

[🇪🇸 Versión en español](GUIA.md)

## 1. First session (10 minutes)
1. **Start new game → Yes, with tutorial.** You start inside the Spatium habitat; Mission Control welcomes you on the radio.
2. Walk out the front door (W A S D), jump (Space): gravity is 1/6 of Earth's.
3. Press **Q**: the scan wave marks each rock with an icon and color —
   sand circle = regolith · orange hexagon = iron (ilmenite) · blue drop = water ice · purple triangle = geological sample · gold star = helium-3.
4. Walk to a marked rock and press **E** to collect. Open the inventory with **Tab**.
5. Pick the binoculars (**3**) and hold click on Earth, a planet or a rock until the circle closes: a card with real data appears.
6. Follow the signs to the **Energy** sector: the battery bank terminal opens the **Power budget**.
7. In the **Hydroponics** dome, plant a crop in the empty bed (E) and harvest it later.
8. Go back inside any module: your suit refills and radiation drops.

## 2. Core systems
| System | Where | The trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Suit (O₂, energy, water, food) | HUD bottom-left | Outside you consume; inside a module you recharge |
| Power budget | Battery bank terminal / habitat console | Off = no consumption but no function; life support never below 50 % |
| Radiation | HUD + alerts | Shelters cut the dose; reinforced shielding costs battery |
| Greenhouse | Hydroponics dome | Fast crops vs. plentiful crops, water use |
| Workshop | Workshop module | Kits, upgrades, helium-3 cell, propellant (costs materials + science data) |
| Supplies & ships | Command center (supplies terminal) | Orders from Earth cost science data; Ariel return vehicle needs 5 propellant tanks |
| Map | M | Icons by color for every building, crew, rover, resources |

## 3. Random events (every 2.5–5 minutes)
Solar flare (real DONKI flares, with forecast) · dusty panels · hull breach · comm blackout · impact flash with fresh samples · shallow moonquake · science request from Earth. Many ask you to **choose**: each option shows what you gain (▲) and what it costs (▼).

## 4. Campaign (13 missions)
1 First contact · 2 Initial resources · 3 First base · 4 Survival · 5 Research · 6 Expansion · 7 Mapping · 8 Water in the shadows · 9 Helium-3 · 10 Maintenance · 11 Solar storm · 12 Expand the base · 13 Going home (lift-off and mission summary).

## 5. For judges: what to look at in 5 minutes
- **Power budget** screen (Energy sector) — the clearest trade-off.
- **A solar storm decision** — Settings → *Evaluator mode* ON, then start a new game: events come sooner and resources are generous.
- **Binocular data cards** on Earth and Jupiter — real NASA numbers in-game.
- Every card shows its **source** and whether it is real, adapted or a game value.

## 6. Troubleshooting
- *The game does not start (Windows)*: unzip first; keep the `.dll` next to the `.exe`.
- *macOS says the app is damaged*: `xattr -cr "/Applications/SPATIUM IV.app"`.
- *Low frame rate*: Esc → Settings → Graphics → Low.
- *Language*: Esc → Settings → Language (the world reloads so signs change too).
